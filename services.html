Modular Kitchens

We design kitchens built specifically for Indian cooking — three meals a day, heavy vessels, hot pots, and strong spices. Every drawer and cabinet is planned around what you actually cook, not a generic layout.

Custom cabinet layout for your cooking style Dekton, granite or quartz countertops German Hettich / Blum fittings Pull-out spice racks & deep drawers LPG cylinder cabinet with ventilation

Bathroom Design

A bathroom renovation is one of the highest-value upgrades you can make to your home. We focus on quality tiles, good water pressure, safe anti-skid floors, and fittings that last — no cheap shortcuts.

Anti-skid vitrified floor tiles throughout Wall-hung or floor-mount EWC options Overhead rainfall or normal shower Recessed wall niche for toiletries Waterproofing treatment before tiling
Starting from ₹1.2L · Timeline: 2–4 weeks

How We Work

From Your First Call to
Moving In — 4 Simple Steps

01 ☎️

Free Consultation

Call or WhatsApp us. Our team visits your home anywhere in Andhra Pradesh or Telangana, understands your needs, and gives you an honest estimate — no pressure, no obligation.

02 ✏️

Design & Planning

We create a detailed design plan with 3D visuals, material samples, and a fixed-price quotation. You see exactly what you're getting before a single rupee is spent.

Execution

We do a complete walkthrough with you, fix any snags, and hand over your finished home. We stay in touch after handover — if anything needs attention, we're one call away.
