We are based in Markapur and serve clients across all of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana — including Tirupati, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Nellore, Ongole, Kurnool, Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar, and beyond. If you're anywhere in AP or Telangana, call us and we'll arrange a visit.
A typical 2BHK full home interior in Markapur starts from around ₹12–18 lakhs, and a 3BHK from ₹18–30 lakhs — depending on the size of the flat, materials chosen, and the number of custom furniture pieces. We give a fixed written quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises.
A single room (living room or bedroom) typically takes 4–8 weeks. A full 2BHK takes around 12–14 weeks, and a 3BHK around 16–20 weeks. The timeline depends on the scope of work and material availability. We give you a clear schedule at the start and stick to it.
Yes — we create 3D design visuals and share material samples before any work starts. You see exactly what your finished room will look like and can suggest changes at this stage. Work only begins once you are fully satisfied with the design.
We typically follow a 4-stage payment schedule: a booking advance to confirm the project, a material-procurement payment before purchase, a mid-project payment when carpentry is complete, and a final payment on handover. The exact breakdown is agreed in writing before work starts.
