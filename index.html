Every Space, a New Story

We don't just decorate — we design each room around how your family actually lives. Here's a look at what we bring to every corner of your home.

01 / 05
01 — Living Room

Where Your Family
Comes Together

The living room is the heart of every Indian home — a space for festivals, family gatherings, and quiet evenings. We balance grandeur with warmth, using natural stone, teak accents, and lighting that transforms with the time of day.

False Ceilings Feature Walls Custom TV Units Ambient Lighting
02 — Modular Kitchen

Built for
Serious Indian Cooking

Indian kitchens are active, aromatic, and social. We engineer every cabinet, counter, and loft for real daily use — pull-out spice racks, hidden appliance storage, Dekton countertops that handle pressure cookers and masala splatter without complaint.

Soft-Close Fittings Dekton Counters Smart Storage Chimney Design
03 — Master Bedroom

A Retreat That's
Truly Yours

Your bedroom should restore you. We design custom wardrobes with full-length mirrors that double your sense of space, headboard feature walls with integrated reading lights, and flooring that feels luxurious underfoot from the very first morning.

Custom Wardrobes Headboard Walls Walk-in Concepts Mood Lighting
04 — Pooja Room

Sacred Space,
Crafted with Devotion

The pooja room is the most personal space in any Indian home. We source traditional stone idols, hand-carved wooden mandirs, and integrate warm lighting that creates a divine ambiance — whether it's a dedicated room or a beautiful alcove.

Marble Mandirs Teak Carvings Sacred Lighting Ventilation Design
05 — Bathroom

Clean. Safe.
Quietly Luxurious

We believe bathrooms deserve as much attention as living rooms. Anti-skid Italian tiles, concealed plumbing, rainfall showers, and fog-resistant mirrors — all selected for durability and long-term maintenance-free living.

Anti-Skid Tiles Concealed Plumbing Premium Fixtures Glass Partitions
