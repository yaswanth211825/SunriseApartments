Room by Room

The Art of
Every Space

Living Room

Where Every Day Begins and Ends

The living room is your family's stage — the space for morning chai, evening prayers, and weekend gatherings. We design it to be beautiful enough for guests and comfortable enough for you.

Custom sofas, feature walls with texture paint or wallpaper, coffered false ceilings with cove lighting, built-in TV units with integrated storage — every element is planned to the centimetre.

✦False Ceilings with Cove & Recessed Lighting
✦Custom TV & Entertainment Units
✦Feature Accent Walls — Texture, PU or Wallpaper
✦Sofa Sets & Centre Table Coordination
✦Marble & Vitrified Tile Flooring
Modular Kitchen

Engineered for Real Indian Cooking

Indian kitchens work hard — pressure cookers, heavy masalas, and constant heat. We engineer kitchens that can handle all of it, while looking beautiful for years without degrading.

Dekton and Quartz countertops, marine ply carcasses with laminate shutters, German soft-close hinges, deep drawer systems, and chimney integration — built for daily serious cooking.

✦Dekton / Quartz / Granite Countertops
✦German Soft-Close Hinges & Channels
✦Pull-Out Spice Racks & Drawer Organisers
✦Loft Cabinets with Maximum Ceiling Height
✦Chimney & Hob Integration
Master Bedroom

Your Space. Your Rules.

Sleep better, live better. A well-designed bedroom isn't just about aesthetics — it's about how you feel when you wake up. We design spaces that restore and inspire.

Floor-to-ceiling sliding wardrobes with mirrored panels, headboard feature walls in upholstered or cane-panelled finishes, and layered lighting — a master suite that feels like a luxury retreat.

✦Floor-to-Ceiling Sliding Wardrobes
✦Upholstered or Panel Headboard Walls
✦Laminate & Veneer Wardrobe Finishes
✦Study Nook & Dressing Table Integration
✦Mood & Task Lighting Design
Pooja Room

A Sacred Space, Designed with Devotion

The pooja room is the spiritual centre of every Indian home. We approach its design with the same reverence as the rituals performed within — using hand-carved wood, natural stone, and warm lighting that invites peace.

Our signature Radha Krishna water fountain adds a unique, serene focal point — the gentle sound of flowing water creating a divine atmosphere that no other studio offers.

✦Hand-Carved Teak & Sheesham Mandirs
✦Signature Radha Krishna Water Fountain
✦Marble & Stone Idol Platforms
✦Warm LED Niches & Backlit Panels
✦Ventilation & Smoke Management Design
Bathroom

Clean, Safe, Quietly Luxurious

Bathrooms are used every single day — they deserve as much care as your living room. We specify tiles and fixtures for durability and low maintenance, without ever compromising on how they look.

Italian and vitrified anti-skid tiles, wall-hung WCs, rainfall shower systems, tempered glass partitions, fog-resistant mirrors — functional luxury designed to last decades.

✦Anti-Skid Italian & Vitrified Floor Tiles
✦Rainfall & Hand-Shower Systems
✦Concealed Cistern & Wall-Hung WC
✦Tempered Glass Partitions
✦Fog-Resistant Backlit Mirrors
