Grand Living Room

The living room anchors the entire villa with a gold geometric partition that divides the space without closing it — light passes through, sightlines remain open. A full-height marble floor flows beneath, and the cylindrical glass elevator is visible as a centrepiece. This is a room designed to impress, but built to live in.

Foyer / Dining · Central

Cylindrical Glass Elevator

A cylindrical glass elevator sits at the heart of the villa — not hidden in a shaft, but celebrated as a design element. It connects the floors visually and physically, rising through a double-height space with marble underfoot and the open dining beyond. Few homes in Hyderabad have this as a feature. This one does.
